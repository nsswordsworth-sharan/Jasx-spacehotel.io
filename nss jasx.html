The JASX Vision

Inspired by the ambitions of SpaceX, JASX aims to make space travel affordable and luxurious. Our space hotel is designed to international tourism standards, accommodating up to 3,000 guests with a total population of 4,000. Located in Middle Earth Orbit (MEO) for safety and accessibility, we offer a vacation experience like no other.

Beyond Accommodation

🚀 Sports Zone & Space Olympics

Experience sports like never before in our zero-gravity arena. Play "Zero Football" where winners can win up to $10,000 or a lifetime membership. Join the Space Olympics!

🛍️ Shopping Zone

We have partnered with top global companies to bring you a unique shopping experience in orbit. Buy souvenirs and luxury goods while floating above the world.

Celebrate Earth festivals like Diwali, Pongal, and Christmas with a spectacular view of the planet. Experience an emotional connection to Earth from space.
